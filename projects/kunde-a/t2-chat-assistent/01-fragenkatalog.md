# T2 · Fragenkatalog für den Vertriebsleiter

**Zweck:** Schritt A3 · Aufnahme. Beantwortet die offenen Punkte, die den Angebotsumfang bestimmen.
**Arbeitsfassung: Englisch.** Auslieferung erst nach Klärung der Arbeitssprache — entweder Englisch
direkt oder rumänische Übersetzung. Vor Versand durch die PM-Person, nicht durch einen Agenten.

**Nicht alles auf einmal.** Blöcke 1 und 2 sind die Priorität — sie entscheiden über den Umfang.
Der Rest kann im Gespräch laufen, mit Protokoll zur schriftlichen Bestätigung.

Wo eine Zahl geschätzt wird, ist eine Schätzung ausdrücklich willkommen — „ungefähr 30 pro Tag" ist
brauchbar, „viele" nicht.

---

## Block 1 · Where the work actually is  ← Priorität

The goal is to take load off the sales team. To size that, we need to know where the load is.

1. Roughly how many customer messages arrive per day, split by channel?
   - Website form / e-mail: ___
   - WhatsApp: ___
   - Facebook / Instagram: ___
   - Phone calls: ___
2. How does that change over the year — is there a season?
3. Who answers them today? How many people, and is it their main job or something on top?
4. Roughly how much time per day does answering take, per person?
5. What are the working hours, and what happens to a message that arrives outside them?
6. How fast do you try to answer, and how fast is it realistically?
7. Do you already use a tool that collects messages from several channels in one place — Meta
   Business Suite, a helpdesk, a CRM? Which one?
8. Which of these channels is the one you would most want relief on?

## Block 2 · The questions themselves  ← Priorität

9. What are the 10–15 questions you get again and again? Please write them the way customers
   actually phrase them, not the way you would summarise them.
10. Which of these are simple and always have the same answer?
11. Which ones look simple but are actually risky to answer wrongly?
12. Is there anything you never answer in writing, only by phone or in person?
13. Do customers ask in Romanian only, or also in other languages?
14. Do you have any of this written down already — an FAQ, a price list, a fabric catalogue,
    internal notes, standard reply templates? Anything at all, however rough.

## Block 3 · "Where is my order?"

For made-to-order furniture this is usually the single most frequent question, and it is the one
with the biggest potential to reduce load.

15. Is it in fact one of your most common questions?
16. Where does the production status live today — in a system, in a spreadsheet, on a whiteboard,
    or does someone have to ask the workshop?
17. Who updates it, and how current is it?
18. Would you want the assistant to be able to answer it, or should that stay with a person?
19. If yes: how would a customer identify themselves — order number, phone number, e-mail?

## Block 4 · Who buys

20. Are your customers private individuals, businesses (interior designers, hotels, offices,
    resellers), or both? Roughly what share?
21. Do the two groups ask different questions?
22. For private customers: is a purchase concluded online, or always through a person?

## Block 5 · Product and website

23. What is on the website today — a catalogue with prices, a portfolio without prices, or a
    configurator where the customer combines options?
24. When a customer orders, how individual is it really? Free measurements and any fabric, or a
    choice from a defined set of models, sizes and fabrics?
    *(This decides whether the statutory right of withdrawal applies — it is not a formality.)*
25. Are there technical product data an assistant could use — dimensions, materials, care
    instructions, delivery times per model?

## Block 6 · Handover and boundaries

26. When the assistant cannot help, where should the conversation go, and to whom?
27. What should the assistant never do on its own? (discounts, delivery promises, changes to an
    existing order, advice on measurements)
28. Is there anything a customer might ask that you would rather not have answered at all?

## Block 7 · Legal and data

29. Where do your terms, withdrawal policy and privacy notice come from — a law firm, a provider,
    or were they written in-house?
30. Does the privacy notice already mention WhatsApp and Facebook/Instagram messaging?
31. The export of the last five months: which format can you provide, and does it contain customer
    names, addresses and phone numbers? We remove those before any analysis — we only need the
    questions, not who asked them.

## Was wir daraus machen

Aus Block 1 entsteht die **Grundlast-Messung** — ohne sie lässt sich der Nutzen des Projekts später
nicht belegen. Aus Block 2 und der Auszählung des Exports die **Wissensbasis** und das
**Auswertungsset**. Block 3 entscheidet über die größte einzelne Umfangsfrage. Block 5 Frage 24
entscheidet über die Behandlung des Widerrufsrechts. Block 6 wird zur Liste der Verweigerungsfälle.
